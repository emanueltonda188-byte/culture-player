🟢 Spotify (official connect)

Connects with Spotify's official Web API. No password ever touches this app — Spotify's own login page handles that.

  1. Open developer.spotify.com/dashboard and tap Create app (any name).
  2. In the app settings, add the Redirect URI shown above, exactly as shown, and save.
  3. Copy the app's Client ID and paste it above.
  4. Tap Connect Spotify below and log in on Spotify's page.

Culture Player v1.2

Honest limits. Spotify songs cannot be downloaded for offline by this app — that is Spotify's rule. Offline here means your own files, which always play with data off. Minutes played are counted inside Culture Player while it is open and connected, as described under Your Recap — Spotify does not share your total minutes listened with any app, and Spotify's own Wrapped counts everything on Spotify's side separately. Spotify songs cannot be made low-bitrate by this app — Spotify controls that stream, and its quality setting lives in the Spotify app itself. Playing Spotify inside this page needs Spotify Premium (that is Spotify's own rule for its web player). Controlling Spotify needs an active Spotify device — open the Spotify app on your phone once so it shows up. Your own files in this app always play at 0 data, offline. Moods are guesses from song names and artists, so a song can land in the wrong mood. Auto DJ talks in text notes only, there is no DJ voice.
